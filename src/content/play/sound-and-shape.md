## The itch

I wanted to *see* a sound I liked — a thirty-second loop of a market at dawn —
without reaching for a literal waveform.

## The play

Mapped amplitude to scale and frequency to hue, then let shapes drift so the
piece never quite repeats even though the audio does.

## What stuck

Looseness sold it. When the mapping was exact it felt like a meter; a little lag
made it feel alive.
