## The itch

I keep rebuilding the same poster grid by hand. What if a seed number did it,
and I just judged the results?

## The play

A short script that splits a canvas recursively, biases toward a few pleasing
ratios, and drops type into the largest cell. Refresh for a new one.

## What stuck

Maybe one in ten was worth keeping — but curating ten machine drafts is faster
than drawing one from scratch.
