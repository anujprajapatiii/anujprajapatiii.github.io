## The itch

Monsoon light is strange: greys that aren't grey, greens that go almost black.
I wanted to pull a usable palette out of a week of phone photos.

## The play

Sampled dominant colours from twelve shots, then built layered gradients that
fade the way rain does — soft edges, no hard stops.

## What stuck

The muddiest samples made the best backgrounds. The clean, saturated ones
looked like stock; the murky ones felt like weather.
