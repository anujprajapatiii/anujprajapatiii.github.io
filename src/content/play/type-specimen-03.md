## The itch

I wanted to see how far one typeface could carry a whole layout with no colour
to lean on. So: black page, one grotesk, and a strict rule that hierarchy comes
only from size and space.

## The play

Three spreads, each a different density — a title wall, a reading column, and a
caption-heavy grid. Same font throughout, no weight below medium.

## What stuck

The tightest spread read best. When every other lever is removed, restraint in
size does more than another weight ever would.
