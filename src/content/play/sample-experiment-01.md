This sample keeps the experiment section ready while the next idea takes
shape. Replace it with a real exploration when there is something useful to
share.

## The idea

Start with one clear question. Describe what you want to understand, why it is
worth exploring, and what a useful result might look like.

## The approach

Document the smallest prototype that can test the idea. Add notes, images, or
an interactive demo when they help explain what changed between attempts.

## What comes next

Summarize what the experiment revealed and which direction deserves another
round. This section can also record what you would leave behind.
