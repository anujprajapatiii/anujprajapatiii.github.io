## The problem

Lantern had six weeks until launch, one marketer, and no designer other than
me. The channel list — landing page, App Store, paid social, email, out-of-home
in two cities, and a press kit — was written as if there were a full agency
behind it.

## The approach

Rather than design six campaigns, I designed one kit: a fixed grid, three
layout templates that each survived being cropped square, vertical, and wide, a
type lockup that stayed legible at billboard and thumbnail size, and a short
motion loop that could be trimmed to any length.

The constraint did the work. Once the templates existed, the marketer could
assemble most of the run without me, and I spent the remaining weeks on the
pieces that genuinely needed custom attention — the landing page and the
out-of-home art.

## The outcome

The campaign shipped on time across all six channels with two people. The
template kit outlived the launch and became the default starting point for
seasonal campaigns the following year.
