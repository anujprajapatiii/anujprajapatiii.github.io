## The problem

Northwind had grown from one warehouse to eleven in four years, and the brand
had not kept up. Sales decks, the website, and the driver app each used a
different logo, and none of them looked like the same company. Enterprise
prospects read the inconsistency as a company that was smaller and less stable
than it actually was.

## The approach

I started with an audit of every touchpoint we could find — 60-odd assets
across sales, product, and facilities. Sorting them made the pattern obvious:
there was no shared vocabulary, so every team had invented one.

From there the work was a single system: a redrawn wordmark, a two-weight type
pairing, a restrained palette built around a signal orange, and a photography
direction that favored the actual fleet over stock imagery. Everything shipped
with usage rules, because the point was to stop the drift, not just reset it.

## The outcome

The new identity rolled out across the site, sales collateral, and vehicle
livery over a single quarter. Sales reported that decks stopped needing
per-deal reskinning, and the design team cut asset requests roughly in half
once the system was self-serve.
