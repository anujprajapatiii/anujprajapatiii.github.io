## The problem

Atlas had healthy signup numbers and an unhealthy drop-off: most new accounts
never connected a data source, and an account that never connected a source
never came back. The existing onboarding was a nine-step wizard that asked for
company details before it showed anything of value.

## The approach

We watched twelve first sessions before changing anything. Every one stalled in
the same place — the connection step, where users had to leave the product to
find credentials they did not have on hand.

So the redesign inverted the order. New accounts land in a populated sample
workspace and can explore immediately; connecting real data becomes an
invitation once they have seen what the product does, not a toll gate before
it. We cut the wizard to three steps and moved the rest into contextual prompts
that appear when they are actually relevant.

## The outcome

Activation — accounts reaching a first connected source within seven days —
moved meaningfully in the A/B test, and support tickets about setup fell off.
The sample workspace also turned out to be the most-used thing we shipped:
sales started opening demos with it.
